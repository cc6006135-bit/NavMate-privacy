Who we are

Nautical Logic provides NavMate, an Android app for true wind calculations, vessel squat estimates, and star and planet altitude and azimuth calculations to support compass-error checks. This policy explains how the app and this privacy-policy page handle information. It covers Google AdMob banner and app-open advertising and the prepared Google Play Premium feature. You do not need to create an account to use NavMate.

Calculation inputs and coordinates

Wind speeds and directions, ship speed and heading, vessel dimensions and displacement, waterway details, manually entered latitude and longitude, selected sky date/time, and display preferences are stored in the app’s private storage on your device where the feature saves them. These values support calculations and restore your settings. Calculations use code and star/planet data included in the app; live sky time uses your device clock.

NavMate does not obtain your position from GPS or request device-location permission. Coordinates are entered by you. The app does not send calculation entries or entered coordinates to a Nautical Logic server, and it does not explicitly pass them to Google as advertising parameters. There is no Nautical Logic cloud account or cloud-sync service. NavMate does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera. Advertising services may independently estimate general location from an IP address, as explained below.

Advertising and Google services

NavMate uses Google AdMob to show banner and app-open advertisements and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable advertising privacy choices.

Google’s Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares information for advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. This includes your IP address (which can indicate general location), app/ad interactions, diagnostic and performance information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. Collection and ad personalisation depend on applicable choices, device settings, and service configuration. A test-ad label does not mean the SDK makes no network requests.

Google states that SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Google processes service data under its own policies. Read Google’s Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from partner apps, and Google’s Mobile Ads data disclosure. Nautical Logic does not operate a separate analytics or crash-reporting service in the current app.

Your advertising choices

Where required, the app uses Google’s consent message before requesting ads. An “Ad privacy settings” control is available near the bottom of the app when Google requires that entry point. You can use it to review or change available choices. You can also manage the advertising ID and available ad privacy settings through Android settings; options vary by Android version.

Declining personalised advertising does not necessarily remove all ads. You can continue using the calculation features if ads are unavailable. Once activated, Premium removes banner and app-open ads. Premium checkout is not yet enabled in version 1.09; it depends on completion of Google Play setup.

Local storage, retention and deletion

NavMate disables Android’s standard app backup using android:allowBackup="false". It does not provide an app-managed backup or cloud restore service. Device-to-device transfer behaviour can vary by phone manufacturer and Android version.

Saved inputs and preferences remain in the app’s private storage until you change them, clear the app’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes local inputs, preferences, and any cached purchase proof. It does not cancel or refund a Google Play purchase, or erase information already processed independently by Google. Once Google Play purchasing is available, a qualifying purchase can be restored using the same Google Play account.

Google controls retention of advertising, consent and purchase information under its policies and applicable settings. Nautical Logic cannot promise deletion of data held independently by Google or a device-transfer provider.

Premium purchases

NavMate Premium is prepared as an optional one-time Google Play purchase that removes banner and app-open ads; calculator features remain available without Premium. In version 1.09, checkout is disabled while Google Play setup is pending, so you cannot currently purchase Premium in the app.

When enabled, Google Play will handle payment details; Nautical Logic will not receive your payment-card details. The app will receive the product ID, purchase token, status, and signed purchase record to verify ownership, acknowledge purchases and restore Premium. A verified purchase record and signature will be stored locally for offline ownership checks, and ownership will be checked again with Google Play when available. Pending or unverified payments will not activate Premium. Purchase tokens will not be uploaded to a Nautical Logic server. Google Play will show availability and price before purchase confirmation. See Google’s Privacy Policy for Google’s handling of purchase and account information.

Messages you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the content and attachments you choose to send. We use that information to answer your request, provide support, and address privacy questions. Email is handled through our email provider. We keep correspondence as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations; you can ask us about deletion. Please avoid including confidential navigation records or unnecessary personal information in support messages.

Service providers and processing locations

The app communicates with Google for advertising and consent services. If you tap an advertisement, the advertiser’s website or app has its own privacy practices. Google, our email provider, the provider hosting this page, and any device-transfer provider may process information in countries other than your own according to their policies. Nautical Logic does not sell your saved calculation entries or entered coordinates.

This policy webpage

This page contains no advertising, added analytics scripts, tracking pixels, contact forms, or embedded third-party media. The hosting provider may process IP addresses, browser information, and request logs to deliver and secure the page. Clicking an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy policy.
